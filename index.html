Meet Momo 🐒

ZuZu's mascot is a little cartoon monkey who keeps you company while you scroll. Stop on a post and Momo jumps over, sits on its corner, thinks for a second and shares a tiny reflection.

  • Hops onto the post you're reading and rides along as you scroll
  • Short, kind thoughts in the post's language, Hindi and Hinglish too
  • Tap Momo for another thought, or tap the bubble to ask ZuZu AI
  • Falls asleep (z z z) when you've been idle, and you can turn him off anytime
